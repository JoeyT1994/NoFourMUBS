Algebraic triple certification: scope and proofs

Status: implemented, with explicit partial computational results. This is
not a completed proof of the global classification conjecture. See the
generated results summary and README.txt. No critical-system root search,
implicit graph, numerical census, or single reference tube is treated as
an exhaustive classification without its domain and covering obligations.

1. Coordinates and residuals

The source atlas is pinned by SHA-256 in the frozen native driver. Its
ordinary seed domain is theta=theta0+Mth*w, ||w||_2<=r. The four theta
entries are the upper-left dephased seed phases, in radians. Mth is part
of the metric: r is not an unscaled four-phase cube radius and is not an
H-only full-phase norm. Every all-K run here uses the original seed
record without changing its radius or frame.

The fresh C1 and normal-form certificates enclose the exact Hadamard
branch as Q(w)=Q0+Uw+H2[w,w]/2+Xz(w). They supply uniform bounds tau,
Z1 and Z2 for z, Dz and D2z, on the original ball. All H phases use its
fixed horizontal gauge. K has six independent column gauges; fixing its
first row leaves 30 phases. Together these give 34 intrinsic variables.

Use the native residual normalization: unscaled real/imaginary Gram
equations and (|H* K|^2-6)/6 for mixed equations. H's 30 Gram equations
vanish identically on the exact chart. The remaining 66 equations are
30 K Gram equations and all 36 mixed equations. Each is evaluated by the
same finite Fourier representation as the frozen moving-symmetry proof.
For phase q(x), the chain rule is
  D f = D_q f Dq,
  D2(y.f) = Dq^T D2_q(y.f) Dq + sum_a (y.D_q f)_a D2 q_a.
For E=sum_i f_i^2,
  DE=2 Df^T f,
  D2E=2 Df^T Df+2 sum_i f_i D2 f_i.
The implementation retains the second term, including H-chart curvature.
Intervals crossing zero are squared as intervals; flint 0.9's x**2
operation on such an interval is deliberately avoided.

For a sub-box with midpoint c inside the original native ball, the
original centre-jet contraction encloses Q(c),DQ(c). Write d=w-c. Then
  Q(w)=Q(c)+DQ(c)d+H2[d,d]/2+X e0, ||e0||<=Z2||d||^2/2,
  DQ(w)=DQ(c)+H2[d,.]+X e1, ||e1||<=Z2||d||,
  D2Q(w)=H2+X e2, ||e2||<=Z2.
Convexity keeps every required segment inside the certified chart. The
global normal form is used when a local jet cannot be used. Barrier
and graph domains must lie wholly inside the certified parameter ball.

2. Signed quadratic support and necessary seed cuts

SCALAR_PROOF.txt proves the seven-group quadratic dual bound used by
scalar_support.py. The four common seed variables and six K error
vectors remain correlated through quadratic order. Numerical directions
and dual multipliers are proposals; an Arb LDL test and inverse solve
prove every accepted lower bound. Finite binary64 proposals are exact
constants, saved in hexadecimal. replay_scalar.py rebuilds the complete
all-K cover and reuses the saved directions and multipliers, without
optimizing replacements.

CAP_PROOF.txt proves the optional necessary seed-cap localization. It
can reduce the region in which a particular pattern has zeros, while
classifying the entire original pattern. Every cut and all discarded
points are justified by a scalar inequality. Iterated centres remain
inside the original chart and use its original derivative certificate.
This is not a smaller-radius substitute for the requested domain.

3. Uniform elimination of K

Choose 30 of the 66 equations, G(w,k), with k in a closed 30-box about
k0 and half-widths rho_i>0. Let Y be a proposed inverse for G_k. On the
whole parameter and K domain, certify
  sum_j |(I-Y G_k)_ij| rho_j < rho_i,
  |(Y G(w,k0))_i|+sum_j |(I-Y G_k)_ij|rho_j < rho_i.
These are uniform weighted infinity-norm contraction and self-map
inequalities. Banach's theorem gives exactly one root k(w) of G in the
entire K box for every w. The derivative is invertible there, so the
graph is analytic in the interior. All full triples in that K box lie
on it. This is existence and uniqueness, not just a conditional bound
valid at previously assumed zeros.

All other 36 equations remain. If L=[I;Dk], implicit differentiation gives
  Dk=-(G_k)^-1 G_w,
  D2k_ij=-(G_k)^-1 D2G[L_i,L_j].
The reduced residual's Hessian retains the additional D_k f D2k term.
Hence its squared residual supplies a genuine four-variable energy jet.
An observed numerical rank is never substituted for the contraction.
At crossings or any singular elimination attempt, failure is inconclusive.
An exhaustive all-K pattern/branch cover and gluing between graph patches
are still required before this reduction covers an entire atlas ball.

4. Finite intrinsic barrier theorem

Let Omega be an open region with compact closure in one certified
Hadamard chart times the K phase torus, or in a certified four-variable
implicit graph. Delete only closed regions in which every zero has
already been proved known. Let E be the squared residual, with all
uneliminated equations retained. Choose rational epsilon>0 and a rational
four-vector a with a_1 !=0. Suppose E>epsilon on the entire relative
boundary of Omega. If Omega contains a zero, the compact nonempty set
{x in Omega:E(x)<=epsilon} has a maximum of a.w. That maximum cannot
have E<epsilon, because the height has nonzero derivative in an open
chart. At a regular level point, the Lagrange multiplier condition says
DE is parallel to (a,0). At a singular level point DE=0 already satisfies
the same equations. Thus a zero forces a solution of
  E-epsilon=0,
  E_kj=0, j=1,...,30 (omit after K elimination),
  a_1 E_wi-a_i E_w1=0, i=2,3,4.
There are respectively 34 or four equations in the same number of
unknowns. No rank assumption about the triple zero set is used.

barrier.py represents the region by an exact rational midpoint partition
of a rectangular root. Active leaves comprise the remaining region;
removed leaves must have their classification recomputed by known_boxes.py.
Internal active faces are subtracted exactly. A coordinate can be made
periodic only for K in turns, with an exact unit root period. Periodic
seams are identified; they are not false boundary faces. All other
exposed faces, including patch ends and known-region walls, are checked.
Closed facet enclosures also cover their intersections and corners.

Each boundary face gets a complete dyadic tree of strict E>epsilon
tests. Only then are the interior witness systems covered by component
exclusions or interval mean-value exclusions. For any fixed numerical
matrix Y, a zero in a convex box X belongs to
  x0-YG(x0)+(I-YDG(X))(X-x0).
Disjointness from X excludes it; invertibility of the proposal is not
assumed. A failed test, missing face, omitted child, exhausted node budget,
or missing known-region proof cannot produce a completed certificate.
Replay derives all geometric faces anew and recomputes every accepted
leaf inequality. Stored status labels and numeric margins are not used
as acceptance evidence.

This implementation handles one coherent chart or graph at a time. It
does not identify different native charts by a guessed transition or
invent a globally single-valued seed height. A cross-chart union needs
separately certified coordinate transitions. It also does not apply an
ambient 110-variable polynomial theorem to a lower-dimensional chart.
The present system is analytic; calling it polynomial would be incorrect.

5. Quantitative local walls

If a scalar support margin is m>0 for a fixed residual direction y,
then ||F||>=m/||y|| on its stated tube. A tube obtained by pruning only
exact zeros does not automatically cover a positive residual level.
Accordingly scalar pattern receipts explicitly mark that missing
positive-level covering property. The boundary checker evaluates all
points of its boundary enclosures, without reusing zero-only Stage C
pruning as a residual-gap certificate.

The product Taylor bound is sigma*t-L*t^2/2 on a radius-t wall. For the
stated unweighted product constants sigma=2/7, L=60, t=1/210, this is
1/1470. Converting to native residual scaling gives at least 1/8820.
This conversion does not change or identify the phase metrics.

For an order-three orthogonal action P, let Q be its moving projector,
sigma a derivative lower bound there, and L a Hessian bound. Equivariance
and the segment formula give
  ||F(x)|| >= sqrt(3)/2 (sigma-Lr) ||Qx||, sigma>Lr,
since ||(P-I)x||=sqrt(3)||Qx|| and
||F(Px)-F(x)||<=2||F(x)||. This controls transverse walls, not tangential
ends of an unglued family patch.

At a crossing, write x=k+y, k=(u,v), y=phi(k) on the normal graph.
Let D=1-q, alpha>=||A||, beta>=||B||, dN=||y-phi(k)||. Normal contraction
and the reduced mixed obstruction imply
  ||F(x)|| >= D*dN/alpha,
  ||F(x)|| >= (1-delta)||u||||v||/(beta+M alpha/D),
where M bounds D(BF) on the normal comparison segment. The second follows
by comparing BF at x and on the graph and using dN<=alpha||F||/D.
Outside {dN<b and min(||u||,||v||)<t}, the lower bound is the minimum of
  D*b/alpha and (1-delta)*t^2/(beta+M alpha/D).
For the enlarged crossing constants,
  D=0.94036028499999, M<=0.168945525, 1-delta>0.11477192799095.
crossing_norms.py actually reconstructs both preconditioners, verifies
the derivative bounds and their norms, and binds the exact-structure
input. On this runtime both patterns have
  alpha < 0.577350269478302, beta < 1.816207894050053.
Domain inclusion and exposed-wall coverage are separate obligations.
The whole crossing exact-structure theorem remains a named upstream
dependency; reading its saved receipt is not described as a new replay
of the complete structural proof.

6. Recycling transitions and full-H inclusions

transitions.py encloses the three choices of distinguished basis and
their swaps. Changing anchor to H produces (H*,H*K/sqrt(6)); changing
to K produces (K*,K*H/sqrt(6)). The phases of each transition entry are
enclosed by rotating its interval into one certified open half-plane;
an interval meeting zero or failing this check is rejected. All fixed
permutations and phase windings are proposals whose resulting enclosures
are checked. The two bases share the same row gauge. Exact rational
matrix identities verify W^2=W, W^T=W, D^2=D and WD=W for the joint
horizontal projector W and the common dephasing map D.

The module distinguishes the joint 72-phase quotient norm from the
H-only 36-phase horizontal Frobenius norm. Its inclusion receipts do not
claim a target local theorem has been replayed. A caller must supply a
verified all-K theorem for an H-only target, or a verified joint local
classification theorem for a joint target. No automatic atlas-chart
transition or classification is inferred merely from close seed phases.

7. Outstanding global obligations

The upstream snapshot reports 2,593 of 2,775 ordinary balls resolved,
with 182 open, and an X-ball result conditional on its documented review
dependencies. Our controlled experiment and saved-witness replay must
be consulted for the additional closure, rather than extrapolating from
unit tests or small regional examples. Remaining all-K coverings,
crossing/regular patch gluing, global source-bound accounting, recognition
review and Stage C's inherited floating error enclosures still matter.
The algebraic modules make these obligations testable; they do not assert
that every remaining positive-level critical system has been excluded.
