Necessary seed-cap localization

Retain the scalar quadratic inequality proved in SCALAR_PROOF.txt, but
remove its four w-linear coefficients from the dual minimization. This
gives, on the entire original complete-cover pattern,
  y.F >= c + g.w + L - tail.
Consequently every zero satisfies -g.w >= c+L-tail. Replace g by the
exact binary64 vector g0, paying ||g-g0|| r, and round the remaining
positive margin downward to an exact binary64 m. Thus every zero has
  d.w >= m > 0, d=-g0, ||w|| <= r.
If m>r||d||, the pattern is empty. Otherwise put
  c0=m d/||d||^2, rho^2=r^2-m^2/||d||^2.
For every such zero,
  ||w-c0||^2=||w||^2-2m(d.w)/||d||^2+m^2/||d||^2 <= rho^2.
All these inequalities, including strict centre inclusion, are checked
in Arb. No actual zero in the original ball is removed without proof.

The original centre-jet oracle encloses Q(c0), DQ(c0). Its global Z2
bound gives, along each segment from c0 to an original-ball point w,
  Q(w)=Q(c0)+DQ(c0)v+H2[v,v]/2+X e,
  v=w-c0, ||e|| <= Z2 rho^2/2.
Both endpoints lie in the original convex native ball. No extension of
the analytic Hadamard chart to the containing cap ball is assumed.
For a K column, shift the old carrier by T_c c0 and include its rounding;
the initial new phase radius is at most ||T_c|| rho+epsilon_c plus that
rounding error. The frozen conditional contraction and symmetry tests
can therefore be applied to these new enclosures of the same original
zeros. A success classifies the complete original pattern, not merely
a smaller requested domain. Failure remains NOT ESTABLISHED.

Cuts may be iterated. The absolute centre is tracked in the original
native coordinates, every new centre is required to lie strictly inside
the original ball, and every new centre jet is recomputed from that same
original normal form. The original Z2 bound is retained. No derivative
bound is extrapolated to an unproved part of a containing cap ball.
