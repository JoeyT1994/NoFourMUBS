Signed joint quadratic residual exclusion

This extends the frozen native all-K classifier without changing its
exhaustive cover, native radius, or family recognition theorem. Residuals
are the existing 96 real Fourier sums: 30 H Gram, 30 K Gram, and 36 mixed
residuals (|H* K|^2-6)/6. All new acceptance arithmetic uses Arb.

A complete-cover pattern supplies
  H phase = Q0 + U w + H2[w,w]/2 + X z,
  K column c phase = v_c + T_c w + e_c,
  ||w|| <= r, ||z|| <= tau, ||e_c|| <= epsilon_c, mean(e_c)=0.
Let L stack U and the T_c, and P be the six mean-zero column projectors.
For any fixed residual weight y, form its exact scalar Hessian S at the
phase centre. Put v=(w/r,e_0/epsilon_0,...,e_5/epsilon_5); zero radii
produce zero columns in the scaled lift B. Each of the seven groups of
v has Euclidean norm at most one. Taylor's theorem gives
  y.F >= c + g.v + v.Q.v/2 - tail,
where c=y.F0, g=B^T J^T y, and Q=B^T S B plus the w,w block
r^2 reshape(y J_H H2). The remaining first-order term is bounded by
||y J_H X|| tau. If n_H bounds H2[w,w]/2+Xz and n_K is the norm of
the six epsilon values, the remaining second-order terms are at most
  ||L^T S_:H|| r n_H + ||P S_KH|| n_K n_H + ||S_HH|| n_H^2/2.
The cubic remainder is the frozen certifier's bound, equation by
equation: Gram weight norm times the mean-zero column-difference
radius cubed /6, and mixed absolute weight times sqrt(2)/3 times that
column-difference radius cubed. Thus signs and correlations are retained
through quadratic order; discarded terms are still enclosed uniformly.

For any seven nonnegative multipliers lambda_c, let Lambda have that
multiplier on every coordinate of its group. If A=Q+Lambda is positive
definite, completing the square proves
  g.v + v.Q.v/2 >= -g.A^-1.g/2 - sum(lambda_c)/2.
An interval LDL factorization verifies positive definiteness; an Arb
solve encloses the inverse expression. Numerical optimization proposes
the multipliers only. The direction and all multipliers are saved as
exact binary64 values in hexadecimal. A positive final margin m excludes
zeros in this tube and gives ||F|| >= m/||y|| there.

This last assertion is scoped to the supplied tube. The preceding
Stage C and column contractions preserve exact zeros. They do NOT cover
every small positive residual level. Therefore these pattern receipts
alone do not prove a positive residual lower bound on a whole boundary
cap. The receipt explicitly records positive_level_cover_certified=false.

Every failed numerical proposal, ambiguous inequality, indefinite dual,
source change, exhausted cover or resource limit remains unresolved.
The critical-point method is never used by this driver to discard an
individual pattern. No global Conjecture 1 conclusion follows unless all
original seed balls, the X-ball and Tao's exceptional class are accounted
for with valid source-bound certificates and the recognition proof.
