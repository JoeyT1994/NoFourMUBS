Intersecting the complex-entry enclosure with its phase information

Fix one column in the exhaustive K cover. Write its mean-zero phase as
v=v0+Tw+e, with ||e||<=epsilon and ||v-v0||<=p, on the original ball
||w||<=r. The complex-entry representation is K=k0+Bz, where k0=exp(i v0),
z comprises the unit seed vector w/r and the six complex error vectors.
The construction in CHORDAL_PROOF.txt supplies these unit-ball bounds.

Three further quadratic inequalities hold for each column:
  ||Bz|| <= p,
  ||Re(diag(conj(k0)) Bz)|| <= p^2/2,
  ||Im(diag(conj(k0)) Bz)-Tw|| <= epsilon+p^3/6.
Indeed, componentwise these three vectors are exp(i delta)-1,
cos(delta)-1, and sin(delta)-Tw, where delta=v-v0. The real inequalities
|exp(i x)-1|<=|x|, |cos(x)-1|<=x^2/2 and |sin(x)-x|<=|x|^3/6 give the
displayed Euclidean bounds. The last one also uses delta-Tw=e.
These bounds hold for every covered completing column and impose no
existence assumption. Each can be written z^T E_j z<=1. Together with
the seven original unit balls, they give 25 quadratic constraints.

Take a real combination of the 102 residual equations, retaining the
contracted H-jet remainder from TIGHT_CHORDAL_PROOF.txt. At a completing
basis its quadratic part P(z)=c+g^T z+z^T Qz/2 has |P(z)|<=t, where t is
the verified remainder bound. For arbitrary nonnegative multipliers
lambda_j, put A=Q+sum_j lambda_j E_j. If A is positive definite, then
  P(z) >= c-(sum_j lambda_j)/2-g^T A^{-1}g/2.
This follows by adding (lambda_j/2)(z^T E_j z-1), which is nonpositive
on the domain, and completing the square. If this lower bound exceeds
t, there is no completing basis in the tube.

Numerical conic optimization proposes the direction and multipliers.
Acceptance recomputes all coefficients and constraints in Arb, verifies
positive definiteness by interval LDL, and checks a strictly positive
lower margin. No numerical optimizer status has proof meaning. The
original seed radius and the exhaustive K cover are unchanged.
