Transferring the all-K statement across a complete cover

An accepted native atlas transfer identifies a Hadamard matrix in one
chart with a matrix in another chart, up to row and column monomials,
complex conjugation, or transposition. Row monomials act on every basis,
and basis-column monomials change only its ray representatives. Complex
conjugation preserves unbiasedness. Transposition is also allowed for
the classification: anchor the triple at H, then conjugate, so its new
second matrix is H^T and its third matrix is H^T conjugate(K)/sqrt(6).
Thus the existence of a completing K, and membership in the known types
up to triple equivalence, are preserved by each admitted transfer.

We partition the full parameter cube of the original closed ball into
dyadic boxes. A box is discarded as outside only when its exact rational
minimum squared distance from the origin is strictly greater than r^2.
Every other accepted box has a verified T0 or T1 transfer to a previously
certified original ball. The original atlas's orientation inequalities
are checked for T1. No source or target radius is reduced.

The existing affine_sphere_upper routine remains an upper bound inside
the closed ball. Its compensation uses lambda>=0 with B^T B-lambda I
positive semidefinite. For ||w||<=r,
  ||a+B(w-c)||^2 <= ||a+B(w-c)||^2-lambda(||w||^2-r^2).
The right-hand side is convex, so its maximum on the containing box
occurs at a vertex. This proves the bound on the box intersected with
the ball, not just on its spherical boundary. All nonlinear Taylor
segments in check_local join two points in the original convex ball.

The saved leaves are checked as a complete, nonoverlapping partition
of the root cube. Replay recomputes every transfer inequality and its
orientation checks, and rejects an open or missing leaf. A successful
geometric cover is explicitly conditional on the named upstream all-K
certificates. Their exact atlas/source bindings, radii, seed hashes, and
compressed result file are checked. Those prior certificates are not
replayed by this geometry-only checker and are not represented as new
independent classification proofs.
