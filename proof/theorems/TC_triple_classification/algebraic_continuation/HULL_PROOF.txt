Using the surviving bin geometry in a phase-quadratic certificate

Fix an initial tube supplied by the native complete-cover construction,
before any refit changes its expansion point. For a K column write its
mean-zero phase displacement as delta=Tw+epsilon P6 e, where ||w||<=r,
||e||<=1 and P6 is the mean-zero projection. The prior column contraction
also proves ||delta||<=p. We retain this last inequality as a quadratic
constraint in the joint seed and error variables.

The complete cover supplies a finite list of closed phase bins. Their
displacements from this same expansion point have centers d_b. Each
bin has five free phase deviations in [-pi/96,pi/96], followed by P6.
For any fixed real vector a, replaced by P6 a without changing its
action on delta, every covered column satisfies
  a^T delta <= max_b a^T d_b + h(a),
where h(a)=(pi/96) sum_{j=1}^5 |a_j|. The coordinate j=0 is the fixed
first entry. The alternative bound rho ||a||, with rho the native bin
radius, is also valid. We use the smaller of these two verified upper
bounds, and include both signs of each proposed direction. Coordinate
directions and numerical principal directions merely choose which
valid inequalities to retain. Replay uses the saved exact binary64
directions and recomputes each support bound from the exhaustive bins.

Let z collect the four normalized seed variables and six normalized
column errors. The preceding phase-residual construction gives a scalar
quadratic P(z)=c+g^T z+z^T Qz/2 and a uniform remainder t. Every completing
basis obeys |P(z)|<=t. The domain satisfies thirteen quadratic constraints
z^T E_i z<=1 and finitely many linear constraints Bz<=b. For nonnegative
lambda and mu, set A=Q+sum_i lambda_i E_i and g'=g+B^T mu. If A is positive
definite, completing the square gives
  P(z) >= c-sum_i lambda_i/2-mu^T b-(g')^T A^{-1}g'/2.
We obtain this bound by adding the nonpositive expressions
lambda_i(z^T E_i z-1)/2 and mu^T(Bz-b) before minimizing over all z.
A lower bound greater than t excludes the entire covered basis pattern.

Acceptance computes the residual coefficients, support bounds and
positive-definiteness test in Arb. A numerical conic program proposes
the residual direction, slab directions and multipliers. Failed or
unfinished searches remove nothing. The theorem assumes the same phase
lifts for the bin centers and the supplied initial tube. It is therefore
applied only at the initial enclosure of a native cover, including the
initial enclosure of its fine-bin refinement. It is not applied blindly
to a later refitted tube. The H ball retains its original radius.
