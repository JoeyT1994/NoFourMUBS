Quadratic equations in the entries of K

The exhaustive native cover supplies six column phase tubes
  V_c = v_c + T_c w + e_c,
  ||w|| <= r, ||e_c|| <= epsilon_c, ||V_c-v_c|| <= p_c.
The columns of K have entries exp(i V_c). Write k_c=exp(i v_c) and
  K_c = k_c + i diag(k_c) T_c w + z_c.
The scalar exponential remainder satisfies |exp(it)-1-it| <= t^2/2.
Consequently ||z_c|| <= epsilon_c+p_c^2/2. We use the real and imaginary
parts of z_c as twelve real variables in one Euclidean ball. This enlarges
the original conditional enclosure and retains every completing K.

The 36 equations |K_ac|^2-1=0 and the 30 real K-orthogonality equations
are exactly quadratic in these variables and w. Their polynomial
representations have no omitted K-phase Taylor remainder.

For an H column, subtract its mean phase, which changes only its column
phase and leaves every mixed modulus unchanged. Its native phase is
  q(w)=q0+U w+H2[w,w]/2+X z, ||z||<=tau.
We expand its entries as h=h0+D w+C[w,w]/2+R. Here
  D_a=i h0_a U_a,
  C_a,ij=h0_a(i H2_a,ij-U_a,i U_a,j).
Put u=||U||r and n=||H2||r^2/2+||X||tau, using real Euclidean operator
norms. The column remainder is bounded by
  beta=||X||tau+u n+n^2/2+(u+n)^3/6.
This follows by expanding exp(i delta) through degree two, retaining
the known H2 term, and bounding the remaining terms by Euclidean norms.
All norms and endpoint enlargements in acceptance are verified in Arb.

For each mixed product write H_a^*K_b=s0+s1+s2+R_s, where s1 and s2 are
the parts of degrees one and two in the 76 scaled real variables. If d
bounds ||D w|| and n2 bounds ||C[w,w]/2||, then
  |s2| <= d p_b+n2 sqrt(6),
  |R_s| <= n2 p_b+beta sqrt(6).
The second inequality uses ||K_b||=sqrt(6), which holds for every
phase-defined column in the original cover. We bound |s1| both by its
linear coefficient on the seven Euclidean factors and by
sqrt(6)(p_b+d), and take the smaller valid upper bound.

The retained mixed polynomial is
  (|s0|^2-6+2 Re(conj(s0)s1)+|s1|^2+2 Re(conj(s0)s2))/6.
If b1,b2,bR bound |s1|,|s2|,|R_s|, its absolute remainder is at most
  (2|s0|bR+2 b1(b2+bR)+(b2+bR)^2)/6.
There are 102 real residuals: 36 unit-modulus, 30 K-Gram, and 36 mixed.
For a fixed real direction y, their weighted sum is enclosed by
  c+g.v+v.Q.v/2 +/- sum_ab |y_mixed,ab| tail_ab.

The same seven-factor quadratic dual from the preceding release bounds
this polynomial below. Arb verifies positive definiteness of Q+Lambda,
solves for its inverse action on g, and tests the final strict inequality.
An accepted positive scalar margin excludes all zeros in the original
conditional tube. Floating conic optimization only proposes y and Lambda.

These are necessary zero enclosures. Some tail bounds use the original
phase constraints even though the polynomial minimization runs on a larger
product of balls. Therefore the certificate is an exclusion of completing
bases in the covered phase tube, not a positive residual bound on the
entire enlarged complex-entry domain.
