Complete subdivision of a closed K-phase bin

Fix an original native H-ball and one closed column bin in its exhaustive
Stage C cover. Keep the entire H-ball, including its stored radius and
frame. Write the five free phases of the column as
  beta_j + (pi/96)(c_j + h_j t_j),  -1 <= t_j <= 1.
Here beta_j=2 pi k_j/192 is the original midpoint. At the root c=0 and
h=(1,1,1,1,1). A split in coordinate j replaces h_j by h_j/2 and replaces
c_j by c_j-h_j/2 or c_j+h_j/2. These two closed intervals cover the parent
interval and share their boundary. All c and h values are exact dyadic
rationals. The verifier derives them from the split axes in the tree.

Let P be the orthogonal projection to mean-zero phases. The child center
shift is s=P(0,(pi/96)c), and its variable phase displacement is
G_h t=P(0,(pi/96)h_1 t_1,...,(pi/96)h_5 t_5). Its squared norm is bounded by
  rho_h^2 = (pi/96)^2 max_{epsilon in {-1,1}^5}
    [sum_j h_j^2 - (sum_j epsilon_j h_j)^2/6].
The squared norm is a convex function of each coordinate separately, so
its maximum on the box occurs at a vertex. The displayed maximum is
computed exactly as a rational before multiplication by pi and taking
the square root in Arb. At the root it gives (29/6)(pi/96)^2.

The signed quadratic calculation in BIN_PROOF.txt applies at each child
center. Replace the projected H center by H_center-s, which makes the
unchanged coarse beta represent the shifted K center. Multiply the five
K columns of its linear map by h_j. The four seed columns, the H2 term,
and the X-normal term retain their full original values. For each H
column set a=||U||r+rho_h and n=||H2||r^2/2+||X||tau. The same scalar
remainder bound is
  2 a n + n^2 + sqrt(2)(a+n)^3/3.
Thus bin_quadratic.receipt checks a sufficient exclusion of this entire
closed child box for every H in the original ball. Its multipliers and
strict positive margin are checked in Arb. Numerical optimization only
proposes the direction and multipliers.

A leaf is accepted only when this bound is strictly positive. An internal
node is accepted only when both children are accepted. Induction on the
finite tree then excludes the entire original closed K bin. A search
limit, an unsuccessful proposal, or an incomplete tree cannot exclude
the bin. Replay checks membership in the rebuilt cover, rejects duplicate
bin witnesses, reconstructs every child domain, and checks every leaf.
It does not use stored margins to decide acceptance.

After these exclusions the existing exhaustive basis-pattern search runs
on the retained coarse bins. The original closed H-ball has not changed.
This operation is an exclusion method for impossible K columns. It does
not itself classify bins containing an actual unbiased column or a
completing basis. A full ball certificate still requires every remaining
basis pattern to be excluded or classified by the existing verifier.
