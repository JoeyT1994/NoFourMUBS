Combining the mixed cubic terms before taking norms

Use the complex-entry variables and the 25 domain constraints from
INTERSECTION_PROOF.txt. For one H column and one K column, the inner
product of the quadratic H approximation with K has the exact form
  s_poly=s0+s1+s2+s3.
The seed vector xi and this K column's error vector eta have norms at
most one. Here s1 is linear, s2 is quadratic, and s3 is cubic in (xi,eta).
With the H derivatives already scaled by the original seed radius,
  s2=xi^T A xi/2+xi^T B eta,
  s3=(xi tensor xi)^T (C_seed xi+C_error eta)/2.
These expressions are obtained by multiplying H0+H1 xi+H2[xi,xi]/2
by K=k0+B_seed xi+B_error eta. No K Taylor expansion is made.

The previous residual polynomial retains the terms through degree two
in (|s_poly|^2-6)/6. Its cubic part is exactly
  2 Re(conj(s1) s2+conj(s0) s3)/6.
For each fixed K column we combine this expression across the six H
columns with the chosen real residual weights. We then collect its
three types of monomial: xi^3, xi^2 eta, and xi eta^2. Symmetrizing the
indices belonging to equal variables does not change the polynomial.
The three coefficient arrays are treated as matrices of sizes 4 by 16,
16 by 12, and 4 by 144. Each term is bounded by its matrix operator norm,
since ||xi||, ||eta||, and the corresponding tensor-product norms are at
most one. Taking norms after combining the equations retains their
signed cancellations. Summing these three bounds over the six K columns
gives a uniform bound for the entire scalar cubic part.

Let b1,b2,b3 bound |s1|,|s2|,|s3|. The remaining terms of |s_poly|^2 have
degrees four through six and absolute sum at most
  b2^2+2 b1 b3+2 b2 b3+b3^2.
Let rho=beta sqrt(6), where beta bounds the H approximation remainder.
Every actual phase column K has norm sqrt(6), so |s-s_poly|<=rho. Thus
the change in squared magnitude is at most 2 L rho+rho^2, where either
L=|s0|+b1+b2+b3 or L=6 is valid. The first bounds |s_poly|. The second
bounds |s| by Cauchy-Schwarz for two unit-entry columns. We use the
smaller resulting bound and divide by six, as in the mixed residual.
The weights' absolute values multiply these remaining per-equation
bounds. The cubic contribution is bounded separately as above.

The quadratic residual coefficients and the final ellipsoid dual remain
unchanged. All acceptance coefficients, tensor operator norms and positive
definiteness are checked in Arb. The conic proposal uses Frobenius norms
for the signed cubic coefficient arrays, which are upper bounds for
their operator norms. The numerical proposal has no proof role. This
method retains the original H radius and every K in the prior tube.
