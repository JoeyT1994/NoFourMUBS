Necessary seed caps from a scalar quadratic dual

Use the residual polynomials, remainder bound and domain constraints of
CUBIC_PROOF.txt. Write z=(xi,eta_1,...,eta_6), where xi=w/r is the original
normalized seed coordinate. For an arbitrary fixed real center a, set
phi(z)=||xi-a||^2=phi_c+phi_g^T z+z^T phi_Q z/2.

A real combination of the residual equations has quadratic part
P(z)=c+g^T z+z^T Qz/2 and verified absolute remainder t. Every completing
basis in this pattern therefore satisfies P(z)<=t. Apply the ellipsoid
quadratic dual to P-phi, obtaining a verified lower bound
  L=c-phi_c+dual_lower(Q-phi_Q,g-phi_g).
It follows that phi(z)<=t-L for every completing basis in the pattern.
If t-L<0, the pattern is empty. Otherwise this gives the necessary cap
  ||w-r a|| <= r sqrt(t-L).
The cap concerns all possible zeros in the original pattern. It need
not contain the whole original H ball, and replacing a complete domain
by this cap is justified only together with this certificate. No triple
outside the cap may be discarded on the basis of a numerical proposal.

The proposed center and residual coefficients are finite binary64 values.
Acceptance recomputes the objective, residual coefficients, signed cubic
bound, positive-definiteness test and final upper bound in Arb. A later
local chart also requires a verified center inside the original ball
and correct restriction of the original complete K cover. This module
does not by itself perform that restriction or prove a full ball theorem.
