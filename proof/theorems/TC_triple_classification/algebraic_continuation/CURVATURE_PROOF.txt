Contracting the symmetry Hessian with the Hadamard error

The native symmetry argument constructs a constant signed tensor A with
matrices A_k. Its curvature term bounds ||sum_k e_k A_k||, where e is the
error in the affine phase enclosure of the pair (H,K). The exact group
identities, inverse-defect test, signed affine terms, and Fourier remainder
in family_neighbourhoods/moving_symmetry.py are retained. This extension
changes only the bound on that curvature term.

The native normal form gives the H error as

  e_H = H2[w,w]/2 + X z,     ||w|| <= r,     ||z|| <= tau.

Each column of the K phase error is mean-free and has norm at most eps_c.
These statements also hold for the conditional local models used on an
intersection of a closed box with the original ball. They do not require
the local enclosing ball to be a new Hadamard chart.

For any carrier B whose rows are indexed by a subset I of the 72 phase
coordinates, form the matrix F_I(B) with entries

  F_I(B)[i,(a,j)] = sum_{k in I} A_k[i,j] B[k,a].

For every vector t and every vector v,

  (sum_{k in I} (Bt)_k A_k) v = F_I(B) (t tensor v).

The Euclidean norm of a tensor product is the product of its two norms.
Therefore the operator norm of the left-hand matrix is at most
||F_I(B)|| ||t||. Applying this to H2 with t=w tensor w, and then to X,
gives the H contribution

  ||F_H(H2)|| r^2/2 + ||F_H(X)|| tau.

For K column c, let I_c=(36+c,42+c,...,66+c), and let
P6=I-11^T/6. Its mean-free error equals P6 times itself. Thus the sum of
the six bounds ||F_{I_c}(P6)|| eps_c bounds the K contribution. We also
retain the native direct-sum bound ||F_K(I)|| sqrt(sum_c eps_c^2) and
use the smaller verified upper bound. The total of the H contribution
and this K contribution is a sufficient curvature bound. We take its
minimum with the original native curvature bound, so the sufficient
condition is never weakened by retaining this additional option.

All contractions are evaluated in Arb before taking a spectral bound.
A failed spectral proposal falls back to a verified Frobenius bound.
The new bound is evaluated only if the original contraction test failed.
Acceptance still requires the sum of the inverse defect, signed affine
term, curvature bound, and unchanged Fourier remainder to be strictly
less than one. A failed or inconclusive bound proves nothing.

contracted_symmetry.py preserves a copy of the native prove routine with
this one replacement. run_curvature_bins.py installs it for native
recognition and for every leaf of a complete original-ball partition.
replay_curvature_bins.py reconstructs the full K cover and every partition
leaf, and repeats the same interval argument. It does not trust cached
classifications or numerical conic solver status. Successful symmetry
certificates retain the native theorem's conclusion and dependencies;
this extension alone is not a proof of the global triple classification.
