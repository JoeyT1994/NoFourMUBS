Retaining H-jet contractions in the mixed remainder

We use the same 102 quadratic residual polynomials and the same seven
variable balls as CHORDAL_PROOF.txt. Only their remainder bounds change.

For each entry of an H column, let a_j bound its linear phase displacement
and n_j its nonlinear phase displacement. The exponential remainder
vector is bounded by
  ||X|| tau + ||(a_j n_j+n_j^2/2+(a_j+n_j)^3/6)_j||.
Here n_j uses the spectral norm of that entry's 4 by 4 phase Hessian,
times r^2/2, plus its X-row norm times tau. We take the minimum of this
bound and the previously verified column remainder.

The mixed inner product's quadratic part has the form
  s2 = xi^T A xi/2 + xi^T B eta,
where xi is the four-dimensional unit seed vector and eta is the
twelve-dimensional unit error vector for this K column. The matrices
A and B include all signed H-jet and K-carrier contractions. For real
unit vectors x,y and any complex matrix M,
  |x^T M y| <= sqrt(||Re M||^2+||Im M||^2).
This bounds each displayed quadratic term. We take the minimum with
the previous bound on s2.

The cubic normal-cross term is
  (xi tensor xi)^T H2^* (B_seed xi+B_error eta)/2.
Since ||xi tensor xi||=||xi||^2<=1, the same complex bilinear bound
applies to H2^* B_seed and H2^* B_error. We take the minimum with
||H2|| p_K/2. The remaining term R_H^* K is bounded by beta sqrt(6),
as every column in the phase cover has norm sqrt(6).

The mixed-residual remainder formula and the final quadratic dual are
unchanged. Every matrix product and norm used for acceptance is checked
in Arb. These tighter bounds retain every completing K on the original
ball, and their saved residual directions and multipliers are replayed
without numerical conic optimization.
