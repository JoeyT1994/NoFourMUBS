# Whole-basis Gram budgets enlarge the 48-ray balls further

This supplements `EXTENDED_GRAPH_PROOF.md`. All vector phase radii are in radians. The 48 centers and quantized-center identities are independently regenerated by the included verifiers.

## Certified configurations

For two orthonormal bases whose rays lie in the indicated Gaussian/Björck phase balls, the verifier proves

`min_{i,j}|<k_i,l_j>|^2 <=1/6-epsilon`.

| Gaussian radius | Björck radius | epsilon |
|---:|---:|---:|
| 31/50 = 0.62 | 1/2 = 0.50 | 1/5000 |
| 16/25 = 0.64 | 12/25 = 0.48 | 1/5000 |
| 3/5 = 0.60 | 21/40 = 0.525 | 1/20000 |
| 5/8 = 0.625 | 1/2 = 0.50 | 1/20000 |

A global vector cover may use these radii minus 1/1000 about the supplied quantized centers. These are conditional ray-ball results; a matrix-neighborhood radius requires the separate global vector-cover certificate.

## 1. Phase-ball errors give matrix Frobenius budgets

Let a possible six-label set be `A=(a_1,...,a_6)`, and let `C_A` be the 6-by-6 matrix with corresponding normalized center rays as columns. For an actual orthonormal basis `K` assigned these labels, adjust its individual column phases to the centers. This preserves unitarity and all unbiasedness conditions, and gives

`||K-C_A||_F <=a_A`,

`a_A^2=(1/6)sum_{j=1}^6 r_(a_j)^2`.

This follows entrywise from `|exp(i x)-1|<=|x|` and the phase-ball norm bounds.

For two actual unitary bases `K,L` with labels `A,B`,

`||K^*L-C_A^*C_B||_F <=min(a_A+||C_A||_op a_B, ||C_B||_op a_A+a_B)`.

For the first expression, use

`K^*L-C_A^*C_B=(K-C_A)^*L+C_A^*(L-C_B)`.

The second expression follows from the alternative expansion. Crucially the actual bases are unitary even when the center frames are not.

Set

`D(A,B)=|| |C_A^*C_B|-(1/sqrt(6))ones ||_F`.

If `K,L` were mutually unbiased, their cross-Gram moduli would be exactly the constant matrix. The reverse triangle inequality therefore forces `D(A,B)` not to exceed the displayed Frobenius budget.

## 2. Exact center-frame operator bounds

The same radial orthogonality tests as in the previous proof yield 52 possible six-label sets for every configuration here. Sixteen center frames are exactly unitary. Each of the remaining 36 has Gram matrix `G=C_A^*C_A` satisfying the exact cubic matrix identity

`(G-alpha I)(G^2-kappa G+(2/3)I)=0`,

where `alpha=2/sqrt(3)` and `kappa=3-alpha`.

The verifier constructs every matrix and checks the identity in the exact algebraic field. Each `G` is Hermitian, so every eigenvalue is a real root of this polynomial.

Put `q=(7/6)^2`. Exact scalar bounds show

`q>alpha`, `q>kappa/2`, `q^2-kappa q+2/3>0`.

The quadratic opens upward, and `q` lies strictly to the right of its vertex with positive value. Hence it lies strictly above both quadratic roots. Every Gram eigenvalue is therefore below `q`, giving

`||C_A||_op<7/6`

for all nonorthogonal center frames. The unitary ones use the sharper exact bound one.

## 3. Quantitative conversion to a minimum overlap probability

Suppose every cross probability `p_ij=|<k_i,l_j>|^2` were at least `1/6-epsilon`. Within any row, the deviations `d_j=p_ij-1/6` sum to zero and are at least `-epsilon`. Thus `z_j=d_j+epsilon>=0` and `sum z_j=6epsilon`, which implies

`sum_j d_j^2=sum_j z_j^2-6epsilon^2<=30epsilon^2`.

Across all six rows the squared probability deviation is at most `180epsilon^2`. Since

`|sqrt(p)-1/sqrt(6)| <=sqrt(6)|p-1/6|`,

the modulus-matrix Frobenius deviation is at most

`6sqrt(30)epsilon<33epsilon`.

Consequently it is enough to prove

`D(A,B)>min(a_A+sigma_A a_B,sigma_B a_A+a_B)+33epsilon`.

This gives the quantitative minimum-probability conclusion, not merely exclusion of exact unbiasedness.

## 4. Finite certificate and arithmetic

For every ordered pair of the 52 possible label sets, including overlapping sets, the verifier does one of the following:

1. finds a cross label pair with the pointwise probability upper bound below `1/6-epsilon`, using the sine/antipodal lemmas; or
2. verifies the whole-basis budget inequality above.

No assumption that the two bases use twelve distinct labels is made.

All center cross-Gram entries are exact algebraic numbers. The verifier uses

`D^2=6+(1/36)sum_{i in A,j in B}|S_ij|^2-(1/(3sqrt(6)))sum_{i in A,j in B}|S_ij|`,

and computes a rational lower bound for this expression. It compares that lower bound with the square of the rational upper bound on the Frobenius budget plus `33epsilon`. Square-root enclosures are obtained by integer square-root isolation. Every acceptance comparison is exact.
