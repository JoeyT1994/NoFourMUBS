## Part 2 — MC1 (strict-corner coverage)

### R(E)=4(Im Ψ(B))² ≥ 0 — VERIFIED
- It is a Laurent identity (|Ψ|²=g²η, 2ReΨ=−χ) and holds on singular blocks. Checked on 2000 blocks, including two kinds of singular block (check1), and on strips via the complementary Gram.
- Hence all-critical ⇔ every Im Ψ = 0 ⇔ every R=0. Lean: `RS_eq_im_sq`.

### Routing lemmas (PRM §7.1) — VERIFIED (by hand; paper-only, Lean takes them as inputs `SingularMinorRoute`/`GeometricRowRoute`)
- **Singular minor.**
  - The circle–line argument gives proportional rows or columns.
  - In the row case, triangle-equality phasing to (1;1),(1;−1) is correct, and both halves of the other rows have zero sum.
  - "Same left orientation ⇔ same right orientation" holds because the inner product would otherwise be one term of modulus 3.
  - Each type is a 2-dim class, so there are two rows per type, with opposite right phases. That is form (5.6).
- **Geometric progression.**
  - e₁=e₂=0 by orthogonality and e₄=e₅=0 by reciprocity, so t⁶−e₃t³+e₆ has two equilateral triples. With v=(1,u,u⁻¹) I checked Â₁=3ω²(u³−1)/u and Â₂=−3ω(u³−1)/u².
  - The dichotomy then lands in (5.5) or (5.6).
- Lean's stronger form only needs "has a 3×3 Hadamard block", and `equivalent_F6_of_block` handles the rest in Lean.
