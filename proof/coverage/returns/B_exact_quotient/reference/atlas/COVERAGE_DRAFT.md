# Coverage: why a finite atlas of intrinsic Records covers every Hadamard class

**Notation.**
- 𝓗 is the compact set of order-6 Hadamard matrices; 𝓗/≅ is its quotient by equivalence (row and column permutations and phases).
- On 𝓗/≅ we use the quotient horizontal phase metric δ(H, K) = min{‖v‖ : v horizontal, H ≅ K ∘ e^{iv}}. It is attained and continuous.
- S = {θ ∈ T⁴ : R(θ) > 0} is the set of strict corner seeds.
- H₊(θ) is the positive-sheet completion of E(θ) = [[1,1,1],[1,a,b],[1,c,d]].
- Two operations on classes matter: conjugation, [H] ↦ [H̄], and transposition, [H] ↦ [Hᵀ].

## Theorem (coverage)

Let W ⊂ 𝓗/≅ satisfy:

- **(W1) Atlas.** W is a finite union of certified Records. Each Record encloses every Hadamard within its region and passes Stage C on every cell.
- **(W2) Open and closed.** Every Hadamard on the boundary of a Record's region lies in the interior of another Record. So W is open and closed in 𝓗/≅ ∖ {[Tao]}.
- **(W3) Symmetric.** W is closed under conjugation and transposition. Records may be used together with their conjugates and transposes, because both operations preserve the quartet-edge property.
- **(W4) Representative.** [K*] ∈ W, where K* = H₊(θ*) and θ* is one local maximum of R. Numerically θ* is any of the 18 maxima, all with R = 389.239.

Then every class other than [Tao] lies in W. In particular, no order-6 Hadamard other than Tao is a quartet edge. Tao is excluded separately, by Brierley–Weigert or ProvenResults/ExceptionalEndpoints/Tao.

## Proof

1. **Reduction to S** (MC5, `MinimalConstruction/Proofs`). Take H not equivalent to F6 or Tao. MC1 gives it a corner with R > 0, and at any such corner H ≅ H₊(θ) or H ≅ conj H₊(θ̄), for that corner's seed θ ∈ S. Note that θ̄ ∈ S as well, since R(θ̄) = R(θ). F6 itself lies in W, by the wide tube or by closedness.

2. **Every component of S contains a local maximum of R.** Let C be a connected component of the open set S. Its closure is compact, R > 0 on C, and R = 0 on ∂C. So R attains its maximum over the closure inside C, at a local maximum of R on T⁴.

3. **The local maxima form one symmetry orbit.**
   - *To certify.* Every local maximum of R on {R > 0} lies in a box returned by `critR.jl`, and each returned box contains exactly one critical point, one of the 18.
   - *To check exactly.* The 18 points are the orbit of θ* under the 144 seed maps generated by permuting the rows and columns of E, transposition and conjugation.
   - *Why the orbit stays inside W.* A seed map sends [H₊(θ)] to the class of a matrix built from H₊(θ) by permutation, transposition or conjugation, possibly with a sheet swap. The sheet swap is harmless because H₋(θ) ≅ conj H₊(θ̄) (ProvenResults/Sheets). So by (W3) and (W4), the class of H₊ at every maximum lies in W.

4. **Continuity.** On S, the class map θ ↦ [H₊(θ)] is continuous into (𝓗/≅, δ).
   - The formulas are analytic on S away from branch loci.
   - Changing a cube-root branch or the sign choice of δ permutes rows and columns (MC3; round-1 return B, `critical/CHART_REGULARITY.md`). Such a change is an equivalence.

5. **Conclusion.**
   - The image of C under θ ↦ [H₊(θ)] is connected, and it meets W at a maximum. Since W is open and closed, the whole image lies in W.
   - The same holds for θ ↦ [conj H₊(θ̄)], by (W3).
   - Step 1 then puts every class other than F6 and Tao in W. ∎
