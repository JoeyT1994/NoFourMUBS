The all-corner conjugacy theorem established in the reconstruction analysis
states that every non-Tao Hadamard satisfies
\begin{equation}
 \Psi(H[I,J])=\overline{\Psi(H[I^c,J^c])}
 \qquad (|I|=|J|=3).
 \label{eq:all-corner-conjugacy}
\end{equation}
The theorem applies to every actual corner, including singular and
zero-discriminant corners. Combining it with
Eq.~\eqref{eq:complement} shows that every three-by-three block invariant
$\Psi(H[I,J])$ is real. For an actual corner with adjacent side $B$, the
side identity $R=4(\operatorname{Im}\Psi(B))^2$ therefore gives $R=0$.
Thus $H$ is all-critical. The independently established strict-corner
coverage theorem, equivalently the all-critical reduction, makes $H$
Fourier-equivalent. The Fourier class lies in $W$, contradicting
extendability.
