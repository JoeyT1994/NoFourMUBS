# Tao exclusion: {I, Tao} does not extend to three MUBs

**Statement.** Among the unit vectors that are unbiased to both the standard basis and
Tao's matrix S_6^(0), there are exactly 90 up to phase, and no two of them are
orthogonal. Hence no MUB triple, and a fortiori no quartet, contains the pair
{I, S_6^(0)/sqrt(6)}. Equivalently, Tao cannot be any transition of a MUB triangle.

# Tao exclusion theorem

## Theorem

Let `S_6^(0)` be Tao's order-six complex Hadamard matrix.  Among the unit
vectors common-unbiased to the standard basis and `S_6^(0)`, no two are
orthogonal.  In particular,

```math
\boxed{\neg P(S_6^{(0)}).}
```

## 1. Exact torus equations

Write a dephased flat vector as

```math
x=\frac1{\sqrt6}(1,z_1,z_2,z_3,z_4,z_5),
\qquad |z_j|=1.
```

Represent the cubic root by `omega=(-1+t)/2`, `t^2+3=0`.  For five rows `r`
of the Tao exponent matrix impose

```math
\left(\sum_j\omega^{-r_j}z_j\right)
\left(\sum_j\omega^{r_j}z_j^{-1}\right)=6.
```

The sixth equation follows on the physical torus from Parseval.  Clearing the
nonzero product `z_1...z_5` and adding a Rabinowitsch variable gives the square
rational system generated by `tao_full_torus_system.py`.

Exact elimination of all variables except `z_1` gives

```math
\begin{aligned}
p(z_1)
&=8z_1^{15}+419z_1^{12}+161z_1^9
  -161z_1^6-419z_1^3-8\\
&=(z_1^3-1)(z_1^6+52z_1^3+1)(8z_1^6+11z_1^3+8).
\end{aligned}
```

The exact output is frozen in `tao_elimination_z1.out`. **This step is an external
computation (msolve 0.10.1) that is not replayed by `verify.py`; see the header and
the re-derivation section below.**

## 2. Why the same polynomial holds for every coordinate

`tao_automorphism_certificate.py` checks directly over `Z/3Z` that the
coordinate permutation action of the monomial automorphism group is exactly
`A_6`.  Its stabilizer of coordinate zero is `A_5` and is transitive on the
other five coordinates.

The diagonal phases accompanying these automorphisms are cubic roots.  After
restoring the dephasing `z_0=1`, a target coordinate is therefore transferred
to another target coordinate up to multiplication by a cubic root.  Since
`p(z)` depends only on `z^3`, it follows that

```math
p(z_j)=0\qquad(j=1,\ldots,5)
```

for every physical common-unbiased vector.

## 3. Unit-circle filtering

Put `u=z_j^3`.  On the unit circle the middle factor cannot vanish: it would
give

```math
u+u^{-1}=-52,
```

whereas the left side lies in `[-2,2]`.  Define

```math
\eta=\frac{1+i\sqrt{15}}4,
\qquad 2\eta^2-\eta+2=0.
```

The remaining two factors give precisely

```math
z_j\in
\mathcal A=
\{1,\omega,\omega^2\}
\cup\eta\{1,\omega,\omega^2\}
\cup\bar\eta\{1,\omega,\omega^2\}.
```

Thus every common-unbiased vector occurs among exactly `9^5=59,049`
dephased candidates.

## 4. Exact finite enumeration

`tao_exact_enumerator.py` performs arithmetic in

```math
\mathbb Q(\omega,\eta),
\qquad
\omega^2+\omega+1=0,
\qquad
2\eta^2-\eta+2=0,
```

using the rational basis `(1,omega,eta,omega*eta)`.  It tests all 59,049
candidates against all six Tao rows, then computes every pairwise Hermitian
inner product exactly.  Its output is

```text
alphabet 9
candidate_vectors 59049
common_unbiased_vectors 90
orthogonal_pairs 0
distinct_pair_norm_squares 8
certificate_sha256 3e4159f4e45947ea8d9161dfd3d5afcfce90a5204cd7a8627ca18529815f1bea
TAO EXACT ENUMERATION PASSED
```

Because the coordinate elimination proves that every physical solution lies
in the enumerated finite alphabet, this is a completeness calculation, not a
numerical search.  Since none of the 90 vectors forms an orthogonal pair, an
orthogonal triple `C_1,C_2,C_3` is impossible.  Therefore `P(S_6^(0))` is
false.  This proves the theorem.

## Reproduction

```bash
python3 verify.py     # automorphism, system digest, factorization/filter, enumeration
# optional, external CAS (not run by verify.py):
python3 tao_full_torus_system.py --order q,z2,z3,z4,z5,t,z1 --output /tmp/tao_z1.ms
msolve -f /tmp/tao_z1.ms -o /tmp/tao_z1_elim.out -e 6 -g 2 -t 8 -v 1
Singular -q singular_rederivation/modstd_fglm.sing
```

The msolve command must reproduce the polynomial frozen in `tao_elimination_z1.out`.

## Independent re-derivation of the elimination polynomial (this import)

`singular_rederivation/modstd_fglm.sing` feeds the same generated system (variables
q,z2,z3,z4,z5,t,z1, including t^2+3 and the Rabinowitsch equation) to Singular 4.4.1
(GMP/NTL/FLINT). This engine is independent of msolve. The script computes a degree
reverse lexicographic standard basis by multi-modular lifting (`modStd`), which gives
vdim = 324. It converts that basis by FGLM to the lexicographic order with z1 last. The
first element of the lex basis, i.e. the generator of the elimination ideal in Q[z1], is

    8*z1^15+419*z1^12+161*z1^9-161*z1^6-419*z1^3-8,

and `reduce(p, L) = 0`. So Singular reproduces exactly the frozen msolve polynomial, with
the same factorization (`singular_rederivation/modstd_fglm.out`, about 9.4 min wall time
on 10 cores).

**Caveat.** For inhomogeneous input, Singular's `modStd` verifies that its output is a
standard basis of an ideal *containing* I. It equals the standard basis of I only with
high probability. The logically needed direction is "p lies in I". So this is strong
corroboration by a second engine and algorithm, not a certificate. Two deterministic runs
did not finish within the time budget: plain `std` over Q(omega), and a homogenized
`modStd` whose zero normal form of p would have certified p in I. No Python-checkable
certificate (for example, cofactors expressing p in terms of the generators) is included.
The elimination step therefore remains **unreplayed** in the sense of this library.
