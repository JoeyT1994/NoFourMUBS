# NoFourMUBS: status, 4 October 2026

**Goal.** Prove that C^6 has no four mutually unbiased bases, by a reduction to a finite seed search followed by
a complete, audited computer-assisted cover. The architecture is unchanged from `STATUS_2026-10-01.md` §1.

**Bottom line.** Every part of the cover is closed or running, except one patch: a thin ring of seed boxes
0.041–0.047 rad from the stationary F3 seed θ0 = (2,4,4,2)·π/3. All pilot ring boxes lie close to one direction,
along the cone d = u1u4 − u2u3 ≈ 0 (u = θ − θ0), where the leading term of R vanishes. The exclusion test succeeds
at every open box centre, and every whole-box failure is a CPU timeout.
