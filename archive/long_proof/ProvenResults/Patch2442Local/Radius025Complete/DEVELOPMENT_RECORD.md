# Construction record and proof boundary

The immediately preceding certificate was the audited radius-1/100 package.
Its original compressed archive and unchanged arithmetic headers are pinned by
hash. This extension does not take its exclusion theorem as an inner-ball input.

## Local combinations

The old eight-residual local combinations did not establish all local inequalities
at radius 1/50. A noncertifying numerical search proposed new combinations of all
94 residuals. The proposals were quantized to integers divided by 2^38, and every
coefficient was exported as both exact JSON data and a native binary64 encoding.

The retained proof uses these fixed rectangular 8-by-94 combinations at 13 exact
references. Every native coefficient is compared with the exact data, and the
full linear defect, higher-degree coefficient norms, radial tail and signed
source term are recomputed. No solver success flag, approximate inverse equality,
or linear-program objective value participates in acceptance.

## Global partition

At radius 1/40, a second-order replay of the old 117,459 closed leaves accepted
72,481 leaves and left 44,978 unresolved. The unresolved leaves were checked and,
where necessary, subdivided with fourth-order Taylor tests, yielding 67,623
accepted leaves. The resulting mixed-degree partition has 140,104 leaves and
maximum depth 26. It has no unresolved leaves.

The retained construction receipts record 627.0814139409999 wall seconds for that
adaptive mixed-degree construction. That is a measured search cost, not the cost
of all exploratory work. Those receipts alone are not the full proof: the public
verifier independently checks the exact complete cover and freshly recomputes
every accepting predicate at its recorded Taylor degree.

Constraint-propagation and alternative global-combination variants were also
explored. They are not part of the final accepting engine. The active global
predicates are the baseline necessary inequalities, with the larger seed/source
bounds and second- or fourth-order common-variable Taylor enclosures.

## No optimality claim

The selected exact radius is 1/40. This construction neither proves that it is
optimal nor exhibits a physical obstruction at any larger radius. A failed
interval test or failed local preconditioner is an inconclusive certificate
attempt, not a physical counterexample. No radius-0.0475 or radius-0.06 theorem
is supplied.
