# Scope of the exploration

A coarse proposal search tried all 270 parameter boxes at raw tolerance
1/16. It passed on 191 and left 79 unresolved. The latter include many
boxes with large a and small/intermediate b. No mathematical obstruction
is inferred from a failed ball cover or graph. Smaller tolerances,
parameter subdivision, additional labels or stronger graph tests remain
possible routes. The accepted data here contain only the freshly replayed
191 regions, with four separately verified tolerance-1/8 improvements.

Discovery scripts/checkpoints are under `/private/tmp/t2-family-wide/`;
they are not proof dependencies. Rational unit centres are retained in
the project. Completeness comes from the integer cover, not the numerical
root proposals or their count.

A full production-width seed probe near the new reference did not pass
its label budgets. The proved local patch is half-width 1/10000. Increasing
its width and connecting the regional radius map to T1' remain necessary;
the goal is not complete merely because the matrix region is larger.
