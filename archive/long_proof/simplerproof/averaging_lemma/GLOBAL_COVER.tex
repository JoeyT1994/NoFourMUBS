\noindent\textbf{Status: incomplete.} The local theorem in
\texttt{PRACTICAL\_LOCAL.tex} is proved. This note describes a complete
partition of the global seed domain into excluded and explicitly open
boxes. It does not assert that the open boxes lie near $F_6$.

\section*{The global statement still required}
It suffices to prove that every Hadamard with $M<5$ is equivalent to
$F_6\circ e^{iu}$ with $u$ horizontal and $\|u\|_F\le3/10$.
The local theorem then gives $d_F<1/30$.
If $M=0$, MC1 gives the Fourier orbit. Otherwise select a corner attaining
$0<R=M<5$, dephase it, and write its four phases as
$\theta\in[-\pi,\pi]^4$.
Row and column orders put the horizontal and vertical squared Gram
moduli in decreasing order. Transposition permits the additional necessary
order $X_h\ge X_v$. This use of transposition is legitimate for the
\emph{horizontal phase norm} target: transposing an equivalence to $F_6$
transposes the horizontal displacement and preserves its Frobenius norm.
It does not assume that the maximum-column projector distance itself
is transpose invariant.

\section*{Elementary whole-box exclusions}
Write $p=X+Y+Z$, $J=2\Re(s r\bar t)$, and
$W=XY+XZ+YZ-XYZ$. Let $R_+=R(p,J,W)$ and $R_-=R(p,-J,W)$.
Necessary conditions for a strict maximal corner with $M<5$ include
\[
 9-p+J\ge0,\qquad9-p-J\ge0,\qquad
 0<R_+<5,\qquad 0\le R_-\le R_+.
\]
The first two follow from the two permanent norm identities. The last
comparison follows from maximality and same-row complementation.
In particular, the exact identity
\[
 R_+-R_-=4J\{2J^2+(15-p)W+p^3-12p^2+27p\}
\]
provides another division-free rejection rule. The complementary value
also has the six-sine-product square expression used by the reconstruction.
All direct acceptance comparisons use outward Arb intervals.

The root box is $[-\pi,\pi]^4$. Cyclic binary subdivision produces
closed dyadic boxes with rational multiples of $\pi$ as endpoints.
Every visited leaf is either excluded by one of the displayed necessary
conditions (or by canonical ordering), or retained explicitly. Exact
prefix-freeness and the equality $\sum_{\text{leaves}}2^{-\text{depth}}=1$
verify the full root partition, including every split boundary.

\section*{Sharper bounds for the seed discriminant}
The exact Laurent expansion of $R$ has 2,083 terms. Pairing opposite
frequencies writes it as an integer linear combination of cosines.
For a cube of half-width $h=\pi/64$, its degree-five Taylor polynomial
at the centre has 126 coefficients. Its uniform sixth-order remainder is
\[
 \frac{h^6}{6!}\sum_k |c_k|\|k\|_1^6
 <4.459.
\]
The polynomial is enclosed term by term; a monomial with only even
exponents has a fixed nonnegative sign, which improves the enclosure.

All centres are on one dyadic phase grid. Arb supplies enclosures of the
finite sine and cosine table. Integer frequency weights are exactly
representable in binary64. Fast dot products are enclosed using
\[
 |\widehat s-s|\le
 \left(\frac{2n\epsilon}{1-2n\epsilon}+e_{\rm trig}\right)
 \sum_j|w_j|,\qquad \epsilon=2^{-52},
\]
where $n$ is the number of paired frequencies and $e_{\rm trig}$ is the
certified maximum table error. The bound uses twice binary64 unit roundoff.
Final interval sums and acceptance comparisons use Arb. Independent Arb
dot products audit every Taylor coefficient at three recorded boxes.
This is an explicit rounding-error bound, not acceptance based on a
numerical Taylor fit.

\section*{Sharper global Bernstein partition}
The complete degree-five Taylor polynomial can be bounded more tightly
by expressing it in the tensor Bernstein basis of degree five in each
coordinate. There are $6^4=1296$ control values. The polynomial lies
between their minimum and maximum, because the basis functions are
nonnegative and sum to one. For the normalized coordinate $x\in[-1,1]$,
the coefficient of $x^m$ at Bernstein index $k$ is
\[
 b_{km}=\sum_{j=0}^{\min(k,m)}
 \frac{\binom mj2^j(-1)^{m-j}\binom kj}{\binom5j}.
\]
The implementation independently checks its equivalent hypergeometric
formula and $|b_{km}|\le1$ in exact rational arithmetic. Products of
four such coefficients have a common integer denominator $10^4$.

For each box, Arb bounds all scaled Taylor coefficient errors. The
integer Bernstein transform is evaluated by a second matrix product
whose rounding error is bounded by the same $2n$-operation bound.
The final extrema include both errors and the analytic Taylor remainder.
Three full independent Arb audits check all 1296 control values as well
as every Taylor coefficient. The forward-error inequalities, rather
than those audit instances, justify all remaining boxes.

This bound removes another 68,712 boxes by $R<0$ or $R\ge5$.
Including the first reconstruction parent leaves 267,409 open boxes.
Applying the same procedure to the canonical ordering differences,
$9-p\pm J$, $R_+-R_-$ and the signed complementary invariant removes
another 206,013. The signed invariant has just 48 Laurent terms; its
sixth-order remainder is less than $9.36\cdot10^{-6}$ at half-width
$\pi/64$. Its necessary bound is $|f|\le\sqrt5$.

rigorously excluded on all six tested cubes of half-width $0.025$.
Together with the complete parent above, this demonstrates a stronger
whole-box method, but it is not an exhaustive cover. The complete proof
still needs all remote boxes excluded and Fourier-neighbourhood terminal
certificates covering every surviving completion. Neither obligation is
discharged by the present partition.
