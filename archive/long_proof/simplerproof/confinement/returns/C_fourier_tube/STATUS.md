## What is fully proved

1. **The complete affine Fourier families.** On the full two-parameter
   torus, for either orientation and all equivalents,

   M = 36 (|sin(2x-y)|+|sin(x+y)|+|sin(x-2y)|)^2.

   If M<144, then nu <= sqrt(2/3) asin(sqrt(M)/12), with a direct
   projector-distance bound. For M<26, nu<0.358337180. All phase wrapping
   and every equivalent Fourier base point are included.

2. **A uniform full-Hadamard neighborhood.** If a Hadamard lies within
   phase Frobenius distance 21/500=0.042 of either complete Fourier family,
   modulo equivalence, then M<26 implies nu<0.55. The matrix may be
   non-affine. F4 further gives nu<=0.451. The proof does not reconstruct
   H and has no excluded sheet or parameter-boundary case within its
   stated hypothesis.

3. **Family local minima.** Every non-Fourier point in either complete
   affine family has an actual within-family descent of M, at every value
   of M. Such a point cannot be a local minimum on the full Hadamard set.

4. **The minimum reformulation.** Assuming the supplied F4, the global
   claim is equivalent to the absence of a local minimum with M<26 and
   nu>0.55. Continuity and attainment of nu, and the relevant compactness,
   are proved rather than assumed.

5. **A corrected projector gauge.** The sharper gauge theorem proves
   d_F<=0.0914 implies nu<0.55. The original displayed F3 formula alone
   does not support its rounded implication at 0.09138.

6. **Uniform physical chart denominators.** Every physical corner with
   R>=epsilon>0 has g>=(epsilon/2)^(1/3), |P_j|^2>=epsilon/36,
   n>=g/27, and rho>=g/54. The complete side identities and both-sheet
   bounds are proved. Local root-branch handling remains necessary.

7. **Global determinant/permanent and moment identities.** Complementary
   blocks satisfy 3|perm A|^2=2|det A|^2-|det B|^2 and its companion.
   Each row triple has permanent-square sum 72. Further signed and real
   moment identities and a complete 66-term row-energy expansion are
   proved exactly. They have not been converted into global confinement.

8. **A genuine limitation on seed-only descent.** An explicit strict seed
   has R=(625-275 sqrt(5))/2 in (0,26) and all four seed derivatives zero.
   A descent proof must retain maximality among all 400 corners. The
   example is not claimed to be a counterexample to the target theorem.

9. **Complete singular-stratum classification.** A Hadamard with any
   3-by-3 Hadamard corner is equivalent to a member of one of the two
   complete Fourier families or to Tao. The same intrinsic condition is
   that some corner has determinant squared 27 or permanent modulus 3.
   Thus on this entire stratum, M<26 implies nu<0.358337180.

10. **An explicit singular collar.** If some corner has determinant
    squared at least 27-10^-16, then M<26 implies nu<0.55, and F4 gives
    nu<=0.451. The proof includes every physical completion. A sufficient
    permanent condition is |perm corner|^2 >= 9-10^-16/13. The collar is
    narrow; no claim is made that it covers all low-R matrices.
