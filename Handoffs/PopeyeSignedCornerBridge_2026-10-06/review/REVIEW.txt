Scope and exact statement

Physical H is the conjunction of normSq(H_ij)=1 for every entry and
sum_k H_ik conjugate(H_jk) = 6 when i=j, and 0 otherwise. This is the actual
unit-entry six-by-six condition HH*=6I, not a polynomial witness substituted
for a matrix. In SignedCornerPhysical.lean:70 the theorem quantifies over every
strictly increasing row triple and column triple. It proves

  cornerSigned(H, rows, cols)^2 = cornerR(H, rows, complement(cols)).

The theorem at line 105 adds Maximal H and leadingR(H)>0 to obtain

  abs(cornerSigned(H, rows, cols)) <= sqrt(leadingR(H)).

Maximal compares every three-column subset and increasing row triple using <=.
Thus all 20*20 positional corners and maximality ties are retained. There are
no seed-neighborhood, classification, H9, quartet-occurrence, extra certificate,
or nonphysical witness hypotheses. Strict positivity is used for the square
root step; the squared bound itself requires no positivity premise.

Negative Gram sign and dephasing

The convention is s=-G_10, t=-G_20, r=-G_21, where G_ij is the partial row Gram
sum H_i conjugate(H_j). For a dephased corner these are -1-a-b, -1-c-d and
-1-c*conjugate(a)-d*conjugate(b). The invariant uses

  p=|s|^2+|t|^2+|r|^2, J=2 Re(s*r*conjugate(t)),
  W=|s|^2|t|^2+|s|^2|r|^2+|t|^2|r|^2-|s|^2|t|^2|r|^2,
  R(p,J,W)=4(27-3p+J)^2(9-p+J)
            -(W-(p-9)(p-18)+(p-15)J)^2.

Full row orthogonality negates each off-diagonal Gram entry on the column
complement. This preserves p,W and negates J. The checked algebra is therefore
R(p,-J,W)=f^2, and the physical theorem identifies that quantity with the
complementary corner R. It does not silently assert f(Q)^2=R(Q).

cornerNormalize multiplies row i by conjugate(H_ic) and column j by
H_rc*conjugate(H_rj). The inherited and new lemmas prove these are unit phases,
preserve Physical and cornerR, and make the selected row and column equal to
one. SignedCornerPhysical.lean:17 proves the literal four-factor expression
H_ij*H_rc*conjugate(H_ic)*conjugate(H_rj), without a physical hypothesis.
This is exactly the expression in native signedf.
