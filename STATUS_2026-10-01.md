# NoFourMUBS: status, 1 October 2026 (afternoon)

**Goal.** Prove that C^6 has no four mutually unbiased bases, by a reduction to a finite seed search
followed by a complete, audited computer-assisted cover.

**Bottom line.** Every part of the proof except the branch-layer tail is either proved or computed and
audited. The work cells are essentially closed. The branch layer is running and is projected to leave only
a thin "shoulder" near the Fourier family. A dedicated agent is building the region certificate for that
shoulder. That certificate is the one real open risk.

---

## 1. Proof architecture

1. **Reduction** (`NumericalCampaign/REDUCTION.md`). A quartet {I, H/√6, C, D} gives a raw Hadamard H. Its
   maximal dephased 3x3 corner has seed θ ∈ [-π, π]^4 lying in the strict domain D0 (that corner's invariant
   R > 0 and is the largest of the 400 corners) and in an ordering domain F. Statement Q: every point of
   D0 ∩ F is excluded or quartet-free. Q implies the theorem.
2. **Inputs A1-A4** (`MinimalConstruction/ASSUMPTIONS.md`): strict-corner coverage, side/completion
   exhaustion, positive-sheet coverage, side formulas. **Now proved** in `MinimalConstruction/Proofs/`
   (MC1-MC5), relative to stated imports from the ClassificationPaper. The dossier replay passes 15 of 15.
3. **External theorems.** E1: the Fourier family does not extend (Jaming, Matolcsi, Móra, Szöllősi,
   Weiner, 2009). E2: Tao's matrix does not extend (Brierley–Weigert, 2009).
4. **Finite cover** of the phase cube, with every terminal box closed by one of these certificate types:
   - domain prunes;
   - SideTriplePrune;
   - the cluster-cover theorem (a common-ray cover with label capacity and a clique obstruction);
   - the signed-corner PSD prune;
   - a Fourier-tube lift (all four pairings land in a proved matrix tube).

`PROOF_OF_RECORD.md`, an upstream draft, gives the full architecture and the release gates.
`PROOF_OF_RECORD_STATUS.md` tracks each of its PENDING items.
