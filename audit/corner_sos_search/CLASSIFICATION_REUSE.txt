1. Diagnose the old candidate before adding more monomials

Run 38842127156 returned Fourier squared norms 4.04410, 4.02927, 4.02937.
The existing Fourier theorem and exact Tao values imply that an actual
Hadamard has F=0 or F=9. The old constraints E[F(F-9)]=0 permit mixtures
of these cases. Intermediate expectations are not contradictions or actual
Hadamards. They identify a useful place to strengthen the relaxation.

2. Split the classification cases exactly

For H0,H1,H2, fix each F to 0 or 9. Exchange of H1 and H2 leaves the
objective on H0 unchanged, so six cases cover the eight assignments:

  000, 009, 099, 900, 909, 999.

F=0 implies non-Tao because the exact Tao value is 9. F=9 implies Tao
because the existing non-Tao Fourier theorem gives F=0.

On a non-Tao matrix use the Fourier sums themselves, not just their norms,
and use the stronger upstream theorem

  Psi(H[I,J]) = conjugate(Psi(H[I^c,J^c])).

Only the target corner and the existing witness's corner frames are used.
Every conjugacy equation has a Gram block containing its monomials and
all support-contained monomial multiples. In particular its squared norm
is represented; imaginary parts are not lost by conjugation averaging.
No unconjugated complementary equality is inserted.

On a Tao matrix every dephased entry is a cubic root:

  (H_ij H_00 conjugate(H_i0 H_0j))^3 = 1.

Also H^[2] is Hadamard: Tao squared is Tao conjugated, and unit row/column
phases and permutations preserve the property. Both facts follow directly
from the same upstream Tao representative already used in the first search.
No Tao exclusion certificate is imported or regenerated.

3. Apply the single-Hadamard results to all six edges

For a<b in {0,1,2}, let S=H_a^* H_b and add a unit-phase matrix K_ab with

  6 K_ab[j,k] = S[j,k] conjugate(S[0,0]).

Unbiasedness says |S[j,k]|^2=6. Thus K_ab has unit entries and differs
from S/sqrt(6) by a single unit scalar. Orthogonality gives

  S S^* = 36 I,       K_ab K_ab^* = 6 I.

Hence every physical quartet lifts to these variables. Conversely the
definitions introduce no new original quartet. All coefficients are
integers; there is no square-root variable or independent row dephasing.

Reuse the same Fourier 0-or-9 equations and the unchanged seven-cofactor
moment-norm identity on these three additional Hadamards. Do not attempt
to reprove them for a different matrix name. Their individual Tao cases
are retained. The new link equations receive their own Gram blocks.

4. Reuse the saved models to add short composition identities

The first six-case run is 38898826837, source 00981072. The non-Tao target
sectors have numerical objective about 0.269080, compared with 0.397821
before the split. The Tao target sectors remain positive and reached the
solver time limit. None yields an exact certificate.

Add the following necessary relations, with S_ab=H_a^* H_b:

  H_a K_ab = conjugate(S_ab[0,0]) H_b,
  H_b K_ab^* = S_ab[0,0] H_a,
  K_01 K_12 = (K_01 K_12)[0,0] K_02.

The first two follow by multiplying the lift definition and using
H_a H_a^*=H_b H_b^*=6I. For the third, H_1 H_1^*=6I makes the left side
proportional to K_02. Its (0,0) entry is the factor because K_02[0,0]=1.
The forward/backward equations, after multiplication by a unit entry,
have at most twelve monomials, as do the triangle equations.

The follow-up workflow downloads each already saved exact model and only
appends these 252 polynomial equations and their Gram/localizer blocks.
It preserves all original indices, equations, blocks and target coefficients
and records the original model's SHA256. No classification model or existing
witness is regenerated. The full exact checker recognizes the additional
identities and retains the same case restriction on its conclusion.

5. What an eventual exact result would mean

The moment/SOS ansatz is still incomplete. A successful job, a small
residual, or numerical infeasibility does not certify any case. The exact
checker reconstructs every added polynomial and labels a case certificate
as CONDITIONAL ON THAT CASE. All six sectors must be discharged before
claiming the result for arbitrary quartets.

These adapters reuse classification/Fourier theorems as mathematical
dependencies. They are not new Lean proofs and are not a single raw-quartet
Nullstellensatz certificate: raw-generator cofactors would be additional
work. The original conditional SOS witness remains unchanged. No atlas
data, generators, checkers, or exclusion statements enter this search.
