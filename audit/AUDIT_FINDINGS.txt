Reasoning checked during this audit
The singular 3x3 phase-block lemma is valid: at rank two, a third nonproportional
row constrains the three unit ratios to a line, so two ratios coincide. This
forces proportional columns. In a Hadamard, a proportional triple of row
ratios forces the other three ratios to be their negatives, which gives the
stated Fourier-family routing argument.
