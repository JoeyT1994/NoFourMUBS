New mathematics:
  t=9-p, z=J^2, z0=t^3(4-t)/4,
  U=(Rplus+Rminus)/2, V=(Rplus-Rminus)/2.
For a maximal corner, 0<=V<=U and U+V=M<5.
  4 z(z-z0)(z+36p)
     = J V(2z+30t^2)-U z(t+6)^2-V^2/4.
Thus J<0 implies 5<p<9 and 4J^2<(9-p)^3(p-5).
If J>0 and p<=7, then the 16-term polynomial
  Q=5[2J^2+30t^2-J(t+6)^2]
      +2J(J^2+36p)[t^3(4-t)-4J^2]
is nonnegative. Its two-variable Bernstein bounds avoid expanding a large
phase polynomial. See positive_scalar_rule.py for coefficients.
