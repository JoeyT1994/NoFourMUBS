# Remaining work and exploratory findings

The gain comes from estimating each D column via C†(D−D0), using sqrt(3)
column norms, and from resolving interval overestimation by a complete
seed partition. A single 1/1024 cube passes without subdivision; the
larger 1/512 cube needs the retained 255-leaf partition. No full production
box of half-width pi/512 has been certified here.

Three of six leading-corner frame classes at F6 are covered. The others
include a singular complementary Gram and broad side fibres. Probes at
half-width 1/2048 hit cover budgets there; no exclusion or obstruction is
inferred from that. They remain explicit missing cases, not omitted
pieces of a claimed uniform result.

An exploratory attempt to unite all matched labels into three side
interval vectors and reconstruct D directly through det(G) was looser
than the new norm bound. Even some smaller subboxes failed. It has not
been promoted or used in a certificate. Sampled successes never supply
a discarded leaf.

The main remaining objective is broader seed coverage along the Fourier
family, with useful constants and a matching T1′ threshold. The separate
family-tube probes retained in T2_F6_cluster_tube_2026-09-29 may support
larger parameter-specific exclusion regions. The local F6 cubes do not
remove the proved obstruction to pairing the existing uniform 1/16384
radius with m≥1/200.
