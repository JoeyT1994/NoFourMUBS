# Follow-up probes, not promoted certificates

An exact integer enumeration of all 400 F6 positional corners, reduced
by the 72 dephasing/transpose maps, found six seed classes with canonical
sixth-root exponents (0,0,2,4), (0,2,3,0), (0,2,3,1), (0,2,3,2),
(1,2,2,4), (2,4,4,2), with multiplicities 48,72,144,72,36,28.
The proved cube belongs to class (0,2,3,1).

The retained script is an exploratory side-cover probe at the other
canonical centres, not the class enumeration or a complete verifier.
Two other classes yield small matched-side errors at half-width 1/2048;
the remaining three hit cover budgets. Extending the theorem requires
reference/conditioning audits, exact constants, and positive controls.
No extension is claimed by these logs. They suggest a next concrete
route to broader seed coverage without reopening the proved matrix tube.
