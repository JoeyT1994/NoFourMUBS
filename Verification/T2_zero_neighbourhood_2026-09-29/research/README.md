# Discovery and failed proposals

The initial arc search used trim pi/48. Its complete boundary-curve roots
passed, but its surface search was incomplete and hit label/cover budgets.
The finishing attempt also remained incomplete. Neither is a theorem
about the full twelve-root region. The narrow and orientation probes
are exploratory controls, not accepted certificates.

The revised search uses trim pi/24; only its completed partition and the
independent 160-bit replay may supply the new arc theorem. The extension
search separately checks the thirteen cubes of the nonvacuous curve.
The stream replay ignores partial trailing JSONL records, checks exact
final key equality and full twelve-root coverage, and verifies that its
proof sources did not change while it ran.
