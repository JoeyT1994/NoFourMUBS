# Exploratory evidence, not accepted certificates

The fixed-reference inverse prototype motivated the proved residual lift.
The optional tangent projection was not used in that proof. Attempts to
raise the F6 amplitude tolerance to 1/4 left uncovered ray boxes, even at
label radius 5/27; this failure is not a counterexample. The successful
1/5 experiment was independently replayed by the accepted verifier.

The full production-width adaptive search is running separately. Its
partial JSONL log is not a parent-box certificate. No production theorem
may be claimed until all leaves pass and exact coverage is independently
audited. The saved search script is a proposal generator, not a verifier.
