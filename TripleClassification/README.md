# MUB triples in dimension six

**The conjecture** (Matolcsi, Matszangosz, Varga and Weiner). Every triple of mutually unbiased bases in ℂ⁶ is
equivalent either to a triple of the continuous construction (Zauner; Szöllősi; Jaming–Matolcsi–Móra–Szöllősi–Weiner)
or to the isolated Fourier triple.

**The reduction.** Up to equivalence a triple is (I, H/√6, K/√6), with H, K and H*K/√6 complex Hadamard. The atlas of
the no-four-MUBs proof ([`../PhaseCover/`](../PhaseCover/README.md)) covers every Hadamard class except Tao's S₆. It
does so by 2,775 seed balls and one ball at F₆. Every triple has a transition in one of these pieces, so the
conjecture follows from three statements:
- **(A)** for every seed ball and every H in it, every third basis K completing (I, H/√6) gives a triple of known
  type, or there is none;
- **(B)** the same on the ball at F₆;
- **(C)** S₆ has no third basis.

This is a separate result, not part of the proof that ℂ⁶ has no four MUBs.

## The certifier: one tree per ball

[`code/onetree.py`](code/onetree.py) proves (A) for one seed ball and writes a certificate that anyone can check
from its files alone ([`CERTIFIER.md`](CERTIFIER.md) gives the format, scope and measurements). The certificate is a
dyadic tree over the ball's chart box. Every node is one of three kinds:
- **outside** the ball, checked in exact rational arithmetic;
- **16 children**;
- a **leaf**: one cell's H record and a whole-cell proof.

The checker verifies every node independently and in parallel. It trusts nothing from the search, and uses no cache.

**A leaf.** For a dyadic cell C of the ball's chart, the checker does four things:
1. Rebuilds the cell's H record and proves, in Arb, that its normal-form enclosure H = exp(iQ),
   Q = Q₀ + Uw + ½H₂(w⊗w) + Xξ, contains every H in C (`cell_geometry`, C1).
2. Rebuilds Stage C's complete cover of the possible columns of K. A column is a unit vector unbiased to I and to H.
   Each lies in one of finitely many certified phase balls, the *colours*.
3. Enumerates every *pattern*: six mutually orthogonal colours, one per column of K.
4. Checks a witness for every pattern.

| Witness | What it proves, over the whole pattern |
|---|---|
| column / pair | a colour has no actual column, or two colours' columns are never orthogonal (column proof trees, `plane_cell_certificate`) |
| joint | no tuple of the columns' certified tubes extends to an orthogonal basis (`plane_joint_certificate`) |
| secant | a moving-frame secant bound with a positive transverse margin excludes the pattern |
| projector | one common row functional is strictly positive on every tube of the six columns |
| gamma | the labelled Fourier score Γ_F of K or of L = H*K/√6 is at most 19/50, so every triple there is classified by the Fourier-neighbourhood theorem (*classified*); or is below the cell's certified lower bound of Γ_F(H) (*ordering-discharged*) |

A pattern ordering-discharged contains no triple whose H-transition has the least Γ_F among the triple's three
transitions. A tree with such a pattern certifies (A) in that restricted sense (*minimum-transition scope*). It
counts through the minimum-transition reduction: every triple has a transition of least Γ_F, and that transition lies
in some piece of the atlas (`ORDERING_THEOREM.md` §3 of the ordering return). This holds when every ordering
discharge uses the same invariant Γ_F and a strict comparison, as here.

**Numerics.** Every acceptance decision is exact or in interval arithmetic: Arb (python-flint) at 224 bits with
outward rounding, exact rationals for cell geometry, and exact integer polar-facet coverage. A search proposes;
only the check decides.

**Check one ball** (the pinned checker is this repository at commit `914919b`, tag `tc-checker-914919b`):

    git worktree add pr4 914919b
    python TripleClassification/code/onetree.py check pr4 BALL CERT_DIR --workers 64

and `onetree.py close pr4 BALL CERT_DIR` produces a certificate (resumable; `code/rusty/onetree_*.sh` run it on a
Slurm cluster, one or many balls per node or one ball over 16 nodes).
