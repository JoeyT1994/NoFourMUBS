# Campaign report, 29 September 2026 (afternoon): the certification cost is solved

**Bottom line.** With the accelerated cover certificate and cover-only scheduling, an unbiased
0.2% sample of 17,291 fresh work cells **certified every cell**, at a mean of 25 s per cell. That
projects to **≈ 6·10⁴ CPU-h for all 8.65M work cells**, down from 1.4–1.8·10⁶ this morning. The
full run has not been started.

The morning report, [REPORT_2026-09-29.md](REPORT_2026-09-29.md), has the no-go numbers this
replaces. The measurements are in [../evidence_2026-09-29/](../evidence_2026-09-29/).

## 1. The sequence of fixes and what each bought

| Step | Change | Result |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Label cap 0.3 → **0.59**, widths to 1/256 (cover.jl). The cap is proved by ClusterCover PROOF.md §2 (1)–(2): capacity one whenever Σ r_k² < 3(1 − 1/√6), that is r < 0.595 for cubes | 12 of 12 locally failed cells certify (at 0.45 only 8 of 12) |
| 2 | Refine only at label boundaries (`frontier_select`) | Sound, but **no saving** on hard cells: their kept set is a thin sheet, so every box is on the frontier. Kept in the code as harmless |
| 3 | Label components by packed dyadic address, not bucket pairs | Label building 12× faster; partitions identical in 12 of 12 checks |
| 4 | Rusty: 300-cell retest and 0.2% sample with steps 1–3 (job 7133916) | Retest: 63% of previously failed cells certify. Sample: 91.2% certified, mean 589 s, **1.41·10⁶ CPU-h**. The failures are now almost all the 2,400 s cell cap: 16 children × (60 s of failed rays path + cover) do not fit |
| 5 | Depth-0 cover attempt (`CERT_COVER0`) | Passes about 30% of hard cells; kept as an option |
| 6 | Warm-start children from the parent's boxes | **Slower** (3–5×): the parent's boxes are fine but twice as numerous. Removed |
| 7 | **The other agent's accelerated cover** (`CERT_COVER_ACCELERATED=1`): [Cartesian exclusion](../../../ProvenResults/CommonRays/ClusterCover/CARTESIAN_EXCLUSION.md), [sharp capacity](../../../ProvenResults/CommonRays/ClusterCover/SHARP_CAPACITY.md), [label refinement](../../../ProvenResults/CommonRays/ClusterCover/LABEL_REFINEMENT.md) | 4.9× less CPU than step 3 on the 12 failed cells ([receipt](../../../Verification/Campaign_cartesian_cover_2026-09-29/README.md)) |
| 8 | **Cover only** (`CERT_FASTMAX=0 CERT_CHILD_FASTMAX=0`) | On 20 cells the rays path certified easily, the cover was as fast or faster (188 s against 198 s at depth 0; 174 s against 241 s at depth 1). It also removes the rays path's floating-point acceptances from the proof |

A float-error-analysis variant of the exact test (FASTVERIFY) was drafted, then withdrawn in favour
of step 7, whose argument needs no rounding analysis. It is archived.
